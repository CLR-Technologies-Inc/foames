# Did the bottling plant get the electricity saving its assessment promised?

Plant A fills soft drinks, about ten million gallons a year, on two shifts. A university Industrial Assessment Center funded by the US Department of Energy visited a plant like it and recommended nine measures: right-sizing an air compressor, fixing air leaks, isolating compressor lines, a variable-speed drive on the well pump, lighting, insulation on clean-in-place lines, destratification fans and setbacks.

The assessment's case study (IAC case study BS0001, <https://iac.university/case_studies/BS0001CaseStudy.pdf>, published by the DOE's Advanced Manufacturing Office) adds the electricity measures up to 320,526 kWh a year. The plant manager says "we did most of it" and wants FoaMES to say how much of that saving the plant's meter actually shows, from a week before and a week after.
