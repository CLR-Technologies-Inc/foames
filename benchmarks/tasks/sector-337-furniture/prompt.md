# Can FoaMES confirm a 20-minute lead time?

Plant A makes concealable, foldable furniture on one shift. Its assembly team often ran out of parts, and units were built in batches that took days to reach shipping. With a Manufacturing Extension Partnership center, the plant ran a 5S event and moved its prime line to single-piece flow.

A NIST MEP success story about a maker like this (<https://www.nist.gov/mep/successstories/2024/lean-game-changer-shortening-furniture-production-time-days-hours>, a US government work) reports a single unit ready to ship in 20 minutes instead of 2 days. Plant A's FoaMES site records the final assembly cell's states and counters. The owner wants the case study written up with whatever FoaMES can and cannot confirm about that claim.
