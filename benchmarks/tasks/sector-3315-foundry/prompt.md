# Does the foundry's compressed air retrofit show in the meter?

Plant A is a centrifugal casting foundry that pours on two shifts, six days a week. A 100-hp screw compressor ran most of the day at 120 psig, with a 50-hp unit for peaks, although the plant needs 200 to 400 scfm at 90 psig. The plant installed a right-sized 50-hp base compressor and lowered the pressure.

A US Department of Energy case study of a project like this (DOE/GO-102004-1726, <https://nrel.gov/docs/fy04osti/33903.pdf>, a US government work) reports 242,000 kWh a year saved. The compressor keeps running when the cell is off shift, so the foundry manager wants to see how the plant's planned hours, and the time outside them, affect what FoaMES measures.
