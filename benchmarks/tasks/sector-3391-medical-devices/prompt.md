# Did rebalancing the line really double its throughput?

Plant A assembles catheter introducers and guidewires on two shifts. Station times were uneven, work in progress piled up between stations and operators waited on each other. With a Manufacturing Extension Partnership center, the plant rebalanced the stations and synchronised their times.

A NIST MEP success story about a plant like this (<https://www.nist.gov/mep/successstories/2025/learning-doubling-throughput-and-having-fun>, a US government work) reports throughput more than doubled. Plant A's FoaMES site counts good and scrapped units at the end of the introducer line. The value stream manager wants FoaMES's measurement of good output per planned hour before and after, and a note on what the counters cannot show, such as work in progress.
