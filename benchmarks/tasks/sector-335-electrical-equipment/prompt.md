# Was the assessment's pump saving real?

Plant A assembles and tests medium-voltage switchgear on two shifts. Seven process water pumps of 7.5 to 20 hp ran at full speed against valves that were 40 to 70 percent closed. A university Industrial Assessment Center funded by the US Department of Energy recommended variable speed drives, and the plant fitted them.

The case study (IAC case study TT0052, <https://iac.university/case_studies/TT0052CaseStudy.pdf>, published by the DOE's Advanced Manufacturing Office) gives the assessment's estimate of 201,360 kWh a year, and says the measured saving was lower. The plant's FoaMES site meters the pumps. The facilities manager wants to know which number the meter supports before the estimate goes into a corporate report. There is no scrap counter on this line; the `scrap` counter stays at zero.
