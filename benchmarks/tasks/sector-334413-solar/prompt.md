# Does the solar panel plant's meter show its assessment saving?

Plant A builds photovoltaic panels around the clock, about 570,000 a year. Most of its electricity goes to lamination, cleanroom air, compressed air and lighting. A university Industrial Assessment Center funded by the US Department of Energy assessed a plant like it, and the plant carried out several of the recommendations but not the largest.

The assessment's record (IAC assessment AM0715, <https://itac.university/assessment/AM0715>, from the DOE Industrial Assessment Center database) gives the implemented measures as 952,167 kWh a year. Plant A's FoaMES site counts finished panels (rejected panels are `scrap`) and meters the plant. The plant manager wants the measured saving per panel set against the claim. A weekly planned maintenance stop is planned downtime.
