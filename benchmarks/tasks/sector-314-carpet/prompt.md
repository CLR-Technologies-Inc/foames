# Does the carpet plant's steam saving show in its boiler fuel?

Plant A tufts and dyes polyester carpet, about 1.1 million square yards a week, six days a week around the clock. Three boilers supply up to 120,000 lb/h of steam. After a DOE Save Energy Now assessment, a plant like it sequenced its boilers and added waste water heat recovery.

The DOE case study (DOE/GO-102008-2537, <https://www1.eere.energy.gov/manufacturing/tech_assistance/pdfs/42460.pdf>, a US government work) reports 93,000 MMBtu a year saved. Its own list of measures does not add up to that figure, so the plant's energy team wants FoaMES's records to say what was actually saved.
