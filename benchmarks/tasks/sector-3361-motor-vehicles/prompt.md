# Did the boiler house work save the gas the case study reports?

Plant A assembles trucks and vans on two shifts, five days a week. Its boiler house raises steam for paint shop and space heating, 50,000 to 200,000 lb/h depending on the season. After a DOE Save Energy Now steam assessment, a plant like it optimised boiler operation, repaired failed traps, replaced O2 probes and fitted blowdown controls.

The DOE case study (DOE/GO-102008-2485, <https://nrel.gov/docs/fy08osti/42136.pdf>, a US government work) reports more than 70,000 MMBtu a year saved. The two weeks Plant A recorded are both in winter, but steam load moves with weather and with the number of vehicles built. The energy manager wants FoaMES's figure per vehicle and an honest list of what else changed.
