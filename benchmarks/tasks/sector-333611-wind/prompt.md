# Does the wind turbine plant's meter show its assessment saving?

Plant A assembles wind turbine nacelles and hubs, about 200 sets a year, on two shifts. A university Industrial Assessment Center funded by the US Department of Energy assessed a plant like it, and the plant took up most of the recommendations, chiefly a high-bay lighting retrofit.

The assessment's record (IAC assessment OK0965, <https://itac.university/assessment/OK0965>, from the DOE Industrial Assessment Center database) gives the implemented measures as 458,772 kWh a year. A nacelle takes days to build, so the line reports completed work content as unit-equivalents rather than whole units. The plant engineer wants the measured saving per unit-equivalent set against the claim. There is no scrap counter, so the `scrap` counter stays at zero.
