# How much fuel gas does the gas recovery unit really save?

Plant A is a refinery whose fluid catalytic cracker feeds a gas recovery unit. A plant-wide assessment with process simulation found that a larger debutanizer and a change to the saturated gas plant would recover more light ends and burn less fuel gas, and Plant A carried both out.

A US Department of Energy case study of a project like this (DOE/GO-102004-1759, <https://nrel.gov/docs/fy04osti/34403.pdf>, a US government work) reports 25,528 MMBtu of natural gas a year, next to $4.4 million a year that the gas alone cannot explain. The refinery's planner wants FoaMES to measure the energy part on its own and to say what the data cannot settle about the dollar figure. There is no scrap counter on this unit; the `scrap` counter stays at zero.
