# Does the footwear plant's meter show its assessment saving?

Plant A cuts, stitches, lasts and finishes footwear, about 612,000 pieces a year, on two shifts. A university Industrial Assessment Center funded by the US Department of Energy assessed a plant like it, and the plant took up the lighting, compressed air leak and housekeeping recommendations.

The assessment's record (IAC assessment GT0630, <https://itac.university/assessment/GT0630>, from the DOE Industrial Assessment Center database) gives the implemented electricity measures as 217,761 kWh a year. Plant A's FoaMES site meters the plant and counts finished pieces off the lasting and finishing line. The plant engineer wants the measured saving per piece set against that figure.
