# Did the lighting retrofit save what the warehouse case study says?

Plant A here is an 800,000 square foot unconditioned distribution center that ships around the clock. Its high bays were lit by 966 metal halide fixtures that stayed on whether or not anyone was in the aisle. The owner replaced them with high-bay fluorescents and added occupancy sensors.

A US Department of Energy case study of a project like this (DOE/GO-102013-3864, <https://www1.eere.energy.gov/buildings/publications/pdfs/alliances/prologis_retrofit_30_percent_savings.pdf>, a US government work) reports 1.9 million kWh a year saved. The center's FoaMES site counts cartons off the outbound sorter (mis-sorts are `scrap`) and meters the building. The operations manager wants the measured saving per carton shipped set against the claim. Wave changeovers on the sorter are Setup.
