# Did the compressor house upgrade save 7.9 million kWh?

Plant A tempers and laminates automotive glass around the clock. Its compressed air came from four 600-hp centrifugal compressors and one 250-hp screw, at about 4,600 scfm and 97 psig, much of it for blow-off. The plant installed lubricant-free screw compressors, repaired leaks and moved blow-off duties to blowers.

A US Department of Energy case study of a project like this (DOE/GO-102003-1657, <https://nrel.gov/docs/fy03osti/33096.pdf>, a US government work) reports 7.9 million kWh a year saved on compressed air. Plant A's FoaMES site meters the compressor house. The plant engineer wants the saving measured, and the time the line's gateway was offline in the after week accounted for honestly.
