# Did six small compressed air measures save what the assessment said?

Plant A blow-moulds 275-gallon intermediate bulk containers on weekdays, around the clock. A university Industrial Assessment Center funded by the US Department of Energy recommended lowering the compressed air pressure from 117 to 95 psi, fixing leaks, cogged belts, occupancy sensors, a grinder control and thermostats, and the plant carried out six of its eight recommendations.

The case study (IAC case study UA0108, <https://iac.university/case_studies/UA0108CaseStudy.pdf>, published by the DOE's Advanced Manufacturing Office) reports 361,081 kWh a year saved. The plant's FoaMES site has the main electricity meter and the blow moulder. The plant manager wants the measured saving, and wants it per container, because scrap fell at the same time.
