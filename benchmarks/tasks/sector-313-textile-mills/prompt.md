# Did the fan drives cut the mill's ventilation energy?

Plant A spins and weaves cotton, about 60,000 yards of fabric a day around the clock, six days a week. The weaving hall's humidity and temperature are held by large supply and exhaust fans that used to run at full speed with dampers. The plant fitted variable frequency drives to most of them.

A US Department of Energy showcase of a project like this (<https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/2014/05/f16/case_study_ventilation_textile.pdf>, a US government work) reports about 1,579,400 kWh a year saved. Plant A's FoaMES site meters the fan feeder. The engineering manager wants the measured saving set against that figure.
