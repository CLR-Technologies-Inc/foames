# Does the heavy equipment plant's meter show its compressed air saving?

Plant A cuts, welds, machines and assembles mining machinery, about 5,000 tonnes a year, on two shifts. A university Industrial Assessment Center funded by the US Department of Energy assessed a plant like it, and the plant took up the compressed air recommendations: leak repair, cooler intake air, lower pressure and a better-sized compressor.

The assessment's record (IAC assessment WV0692, <https://itac.university/assessment/WV0692>, from the DOE Industrial Assessment Center database) gives the implemented electricity measures as 92,823 kWh a year. Plant A's FoaMES site weighs finished equipment as the shop's output (rework is `scrap`) and meters the plant. The plant manager wants the measured saving per tonne set against the claim.
