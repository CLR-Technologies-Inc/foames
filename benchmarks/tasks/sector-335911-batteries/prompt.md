# Does the battery plant's meter show its leak repair saving?

Plant A casts grids, pastes plates, assembles and forms lead-acid batteries, about 786,000 a year, on two shifts. Formation and compressed air take much of its electricity. A university Industrial Assessment Center funded by the US Department of Energy assessed a plant like it; the plant repaired its compressed air leaks and changed some lighting.

The assessment's record (IAC assessment GT0978, <https://itac.university/assessment/GT0978>, from the DOE Industrial Assessment Center database) gives the implemented measures as 695,508 kWh a year, under 3% of the plant's use, so week-to-week variation matters. Plant A's FoaMES site counts batteries off assembly line 2 (failed formation tests are `scrap`) and meters the plant. The plant engineer wants the measured saving per battery set against the claim.
