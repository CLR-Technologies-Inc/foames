# Does the sawmill's meter show the saving its assessment recommended?

Plant A saws, kiln-dries and planes lumber, about 44 million board feet a year, around the clock. Its kilns are heated from a wood-waste boiler, and electricity drives the saws, planers, fans and conveyors. A university Industrial Assessment Center funded by the US Department of Energy assessed a mill like it and recommended a few measures, including boiler flue gas tuning and electrical changes worth 33,705 kWh a year.

The assessment's record (IAC assessment LT0121, <https://itac.university/assessment/LT0121>, from the DOE Industrial Assessment Center database) lists no recommendation as implemented. The mill manager believes "something" was done after the visit and wants FoaMES to say what the mill's electricity meter shows per thousand board feet (MBF) of lumber, from a week before and a week after.
