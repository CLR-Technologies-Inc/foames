# Did the new pump and motor save what the fact sheet says?

Plant A washes coal for about 16 hours a day, five days a week. A 200-hp pump in its heavy media circuit was oversized: designed for 4,650 gpm at 114 feet, it ran at about 3,612 gpm at 107 feet, with less than 59 percent wire-to-water efficiency. The plant fitted a smaller casing and a premium efficiency motor.

A US Department of Energy fact sheet on a project like this (<https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/2014/05/f16/optimized_coal_plant_pump.pdf>, a US government work) reports 87,184 kWh a year saved, and gives a percentage that does not match its own figures. Plant A's FoaMES site meters the pump's motor. The preparation plant manager wants the measured saving per ton washed. There is no scrap counter on this circuit; the `scrap` counter stays at zero.
