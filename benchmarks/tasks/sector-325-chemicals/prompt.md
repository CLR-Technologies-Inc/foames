# Do the steam trap and leak repairs show up in the steam meter?

Plant A makes glycol ethers and amines, several billion pounds a year, around the clock. A DOE Save Energy Now steam assessment found failed traps and leaks across the site, and the plant retrofitted traps and repaired leaks.

The DOE case study of a project like this (DOE/GO-102007-2467, <https://www1.eere.energy.gov/manufacturing/tech_assistance/pdfs/42009.pdf>, a US government work) reports 272,000 MMBtu a year saved. Leaks and failed traps waste steam whatever the units produce, so the site energy engineer expects the saving to show as lower base load. There is no scrap counter on this unit; the `scrap` counter stays at zero.
