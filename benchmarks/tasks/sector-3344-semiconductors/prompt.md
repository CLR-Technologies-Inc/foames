# Can the fab's meter see a 1 percent saving?

Plant A is an 8-inch wafer fab with a large ISO 3 cleanroom, using about 200 million kWh of electricity a year. Its energy team ran pumping and compressed air projects under a formal energy management system.

A US Department of Energy case study of a fab like this (DOE/EE-0579, <https://www1.eere.energy.gov/manufacturing/tech_assistance/pdfs/freescale_case_study.pdf>, a US government work) reports completed projects saving 2.55 million kWh a year, and a much larger cumulative figure on another basis. Against a fab's total use that saving is small, and wafer output varies more than that from week to week. The energy manager wants FoaMES's measurement and an honest statement of how much the measurement can say.
