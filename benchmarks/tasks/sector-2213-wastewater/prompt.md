# Does the aeration change save 2.8 million kWh a year?

Plant A treats about 80 million gallons of municipal wastewater a day. Its eight aeration tanks are fed by thirty-two 100-hp blowers. A process optimisation study showed the plant could meet its oxygen demand with fewer blowers, and the operators cut the blowers in service from 21 to 13.

A US Department of Energy performance spotlight on a project like this (DOE/GO-102005-2136, <https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1218544>, a US government work) reports about 2.81 million kWh a year saved. Plant A's FoaMES site meters the blower building and records treated flow as the line's output. Flow follows rainfall, not a production plan, so the superintendent wants the saving per million gallons. There is no scrap counter; the `scrap` counter stays at zero.
