# Did the tire plant's steam trap programme save the gas its assessment estimated?

Plant A builds and cures off-road tires, about a million a year, on two shifts. Its curing presses run on steam from a gas-fired boiler house, and many of its steam traps had failed open. A university Industrial Assessment Center funded by the US Department of Energy assessed a plant like it; the plant set up a steam trap survey and repair programme, and a predictive maintenance programme for its presses.

The assessment's record (IAC assessment TT0317, <https://itac.university/assessment/TT0317>, from the DOE Industrial Assessment Center database) gives the steam trap saving as $79,406 of gas a year, about 15,786 MMBtu at its gas price. Plant A's FoaMES site counts cured tires (rejects are `scrap`) and meters the boiler house gas. The energy manager wants the measured saving per tire set against that figure.
