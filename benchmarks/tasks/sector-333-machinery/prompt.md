# Does the engine plant's energy per engine fall as far as its certification says?

Plant A machines cast iron, assembles, paints and tests diesel engines, on three shifts, six days a week. It set up an ISO 50001 energy management system and worked through its compressed air, lighting and process heating. Its improvement was measured with a regression model and certified under the US Department of Energy's Superior Energy Performance programme.

DOE slides on a plant like this (<https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/2015/12/f27/Cummins%20MV%20Slides%20r3_17Nov2016.pdf>, published by the DOE) report a 12.6 percent energy performance improvement against the baseline year. That figure is normalised for weather and production; FoaMES's own check is simpler, energy per good engine. The plant's FoaMES site meters site energy in MMBtu and counts engines off the assembly and test line. The energy manager wants FoaMES's figure next to the certified one.
