# Did optimising compressed air at the forge save what DOE reported?

Plant A hot-forges steel parts on two shifts, five days a week. Air drives the press clutches, the die blow-off and the trim presses. The plant reworked its compressed air system: controls, storage, leak repair and a lower header pressure.

A US Department of Energy case study of a project like this (DOE/GO-102003-1654, <https://www.osti.gov/biblio/15003882>, a US government work) reports 820,000 kWh a year saved. Plant A's FoaMES site meters the compressor room and counts forgings at press line 4. The plant engineer wants the measured saving and a write-up for the corporate energy report.
