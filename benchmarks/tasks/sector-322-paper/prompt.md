# Did the mill's steam projects save the gas the case study reports?

Plant A makes coated paper, about 700 tons a day, around the clock with a weekly wash-up. Its boilers raise about 166,000 lb/h of steam at 200 psig. After a DOE Save Energy Now assessment, a mill like it trimmed boiler oxygen, recovered blowdown heat, extended a low-pressure steam header and repaired traps.

The DOE case study (DOE/GO-102008-2525, <https://www1.eere.energy.gov/manufacturing/tech_assistance/pdfs/42352.pdf>, a US government work) reports about 58,200 MMBtu of natural gas a year saved. The mill's production rose a lot over the same years, so the energy manager wants the saving measured per ton, not from the gas bill.
