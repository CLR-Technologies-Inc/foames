# Did tuning the melting furnace save what the case study says?

Plant A melts aluminum in a reverberatory furnace for its extrusion presses, six days a week around the clock. The furnace ran with about 5 percent excess oxygen at high fire and leaked heat through worn door sills. The plant tuned the burners and repaired the doors.

A US Department of Energy case study of a project like this (DOE/GO-102008-2561, <https://www1.eere.energy.gov/manufacturing/tech_assistance/pdfs/42782.pdf>, a US government work) reports about 45,000 MMBtu a year saved, more than the assessment had projected for the same measures. The melt shop supervisor wants FoaMES's records to say which figure the furnace's own gas meter supports.
