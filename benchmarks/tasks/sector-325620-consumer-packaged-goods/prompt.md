# Does the beauty products plant's meter show its assessment saving?

Plant A mixes and fills health and beauty products, about 198 tons a year, on one shift. A university Industrial Assessment Center funded by the US Department of Energy assessed a plant like it. The plant started switching off mixers, heaters and conveyors when not in use, fixed compressed air leaks and claimed a tax exemption on its energy purchases.

The assessment's record (IAC assessment UU0241, <https://itac.university/assessment/UU0241>, from the DOE Industrial Assessment Center database) gives the implemented electricity measures as 67,490 kWh a year. Plant A's FoaMES site weighs finished product off the filling line (rejected fills are `scrap`) and meters the plant. The owner wants the measured saving per kilogram set against the claim; a tax exemption saves money but no energy.
