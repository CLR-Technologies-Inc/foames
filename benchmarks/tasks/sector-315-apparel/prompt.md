# Does the sewing plant's meter show its assessment saving?

Plant A cuts and sews tailored garments, about 400,000 units a year, on one shift. Its electricity goes mostly to lighting, heating and cooling the building and compressed air for the pressing equipment, much of it running when nobody is sewing. A university Industrial Assessment Center funded by the US Department of Energy assessed a plant like it, and the plant carried out nearly all the recommendations.

The assessment's record (IAC assessment UK1905, <https://itac.university/assessment/UK1905>, from the DOE Industrial Assessment Center database) gives the implemented measures as 251,963 kWh a year, close to a third of the plant's 859,520 kWh. Plant A's FoaMES site meters the plant and counts finished garments. The plant manager wants the measured saving per garment set against that figure.
