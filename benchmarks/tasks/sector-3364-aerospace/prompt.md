# Did total productive maintenance lift the lathe cell's OEE to 45 percent?

Plant A machines precision tube and pipe assemblies for aerospace customers on two shifts. Breakdowns on a CNC lathe cell limited what it could ship to a key customer. With help from a Manufacturing Extension Partnership center, the plant mapped its maintenance value stream, trained operators in total productive maintenance, and added planned maintenance windows.

A NIST MEP success story about a cell like this (<https://www.nist.gov/mep/successstories/2022/total-productive-maintenance-reduces-equipment-downtime-and-lost-capacity>, a US government work) reports OEE rising from 39 to 45 percent. The after week includes a planned maintenance window. The plant manager wants FoaMES's own OEE for both weeks and asks whether the 45 percent holds.
