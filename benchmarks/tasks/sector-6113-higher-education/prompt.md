# Did the campus energy project save 37 percent?

Plant A here is a college campus of more than 100,000 square feet: offices, a gymnasium, a theatre, a library, a broadcasting studio and laboratories. The college recommissioned its buildings and upgraded HVAC, heating, chillers and lighting.

A US Department of Energy Better Buildings showcase of a project like this (<https://betterbuildingssolutioncenter.energy.gov/showcase-projects/community-college-allegheny-county-ccac-south-campus-energy-project>) reports a 37% energy saving. The campus's FoaMES site reports occupied classroom-hours from the room booking system as its output and the campus energy meter in MMBtu. The facilities director wants FoaMES's reduction in energy per classroom-hour next to the claim. Hours with rooms booked but empty count as Idle, and a building closed for a fault is unplanned downtime; there is no scrap counter, so the `scrap` counter stays at zero.
