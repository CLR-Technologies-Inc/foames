# Did the baghouse fan drive save energy, or did the kiln just make more clinker?

Plant A is a cement plant whose kiln dust collector is served by a 125-hp belt-driven centrifugal fan. The plant fitted a mechanical adjustable speed drive to it, which also let the kiln run a little harder.

A US Department of Energy case study of a project like this (DOE/GO-102002-1599, <https://nrel.gov/docs/fy02osti/32107.pdf>, a US government work) reports 175,000 kWh a year saved and 3.5 percent more production. Plant A's FoaMES site meters the fan. The plant manager wants the energy saving measured, and wants to know how much of it is only the higher output spreading the fan's energy over more tons.
