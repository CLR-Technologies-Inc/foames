## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-16

The second public alpha. It adds quality and compliance modules (PFMEA, process validation and equipment qualification, SOPs, work instructions, EHS, commissioning, enterprise risk), production scheduling and cycle-time projections, shop-floor inventory, P&ID support and a live topology graph, read-only mode for connectors, life-sciences program management, agent skills, an agent benchmark and a user wiki. FoaMES runs on Frappe v15 and v16 and is licensed under Apache-2.0. Interfaces, DocTypes and MCP tools may still change between 0.x minor versions.

### Added

- Production and planning
  - Automatic and manual time projections: Cycle Time Standards, projections for a quantity, automation scenarios and a batch API for schedulers (#47)
  - Finite-capacity production scheduling from shift calendars, maintenance and cycle times, with dispatching rules, a Gantt view and ERP demand (#49)
  - Shop-floor inventory, material queues, dry-line forecasting and Material Processing Complexity (MPC) (#72)
- Quality and compliance
  - SOP management: controlled revisions, sign-off, periodic review, acknowledgement and a training matrix (#48)
  - Process validation and equipment qualification: IQ, OQ, PQ, PPQ and CPV (#50)
  - PFMEA with AIAG-VDA action priority and a floor-observed occurrence check (#51)
  - Work instructions: revision-controlled steps run at the station, with captures, limits and sign-offs (#53)
  - Enterprise risk management: risk register, hierarchy of controls, tolerance and review (#66)
  - Life sciences: NPI under a phase review discipline, requirements tracing, MSAT tech transfer and process development simulation (#67)
- Plant and equipment
  - Equipment commissioning: FAT and SAT, loop checks, punch list and handover (#54)
  - Environmental health and safety: incidents, permits and LOTO, hazards and exposure (#55)
  - P&ID support: a tag register, DEXPI and CSV import, and a live P&ID view (#57)
  - A live topology graph with a data layer over the physical layer, Signal Bindings, collectors and Mermaid and Grafana exports (#59)
  - Equipment Complexity Level (ECL) on the Machine, with equipment and materials agent skills (#77)
- People
  - Operator recognition: kudos, credited ideas, milestones and team highlights on the Floor View (#56)
- Integrations
  - Maintenance Work Orders post to ERPNext as Asset Repairs (#43)
  - A read-only process historian bridge for PI, InfluxDB, Ignition, TimescaleDB and SQL (#52)
  - Read-only mode: connectors read first, and writes wait for an operator's e-signature, with a hash-chained audit log (#64)
- Agents and benchmarks
  - An automated benchmark of coding agents on FoaMES tasks (#44), one benchmark case study per industrial sector (#70), and runs on free models with dated reports and a reference slider (#75)
  - Secure read-only discovery skills for site-specific vendor installations (#65)

### Changed

- One Apache-2.0 SPDX header on every source file, checked in CI, and a licensing guide (#62)
- `flit-core` may be any version from 3.4 up to, not including, 5 (#68)
- The version now comes from `foames/__init__.py` and is reported as 0.2.0 (it read 0.0.1 in the v0.1.0 release)

### Security

- A disclosure guard for the public repository: gitleaks, a private-word and personal-email check in CI and pre-commit, a wider `.gitignore`, wider Dependabot coverage and a `SECURITY.md` (#76)

### Documentation

- A Diátaxis wiki for new users with a workflow that syncs it, and a Docker smoke test of the documented steps (#69), plus the Material Processing Complexity page (#73)
- A pumpjack solar case-study white paper (#45)
- A GitHub stars badge and Star History chart in the README (#61)
- Versioning, pre-release markings and changelog fragments ([docs/versioning.md](docs/versioning.md))

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-15
