The manufacturing-data track has a CNC equipment study: a public CNC lathe dataset (62 parts, eight sensors, three planted deviations) and a three-machine CNC milling vibration dataset, checked for SPC limits and signals, energy per part and the ECL profile each machine gives an equipment agent, with a reported study of which sensor sees which deviation, false alarms and limit transfer between machines. Kaggle sources are fetched with the license Kaggle states checked first, or imported from a browser download with `foames-bench data import`.
